Nobody else does this

Run three phases,
not five.

Every methodology tool assumes you run the whole method. Real programmes don't. Choose the phases this programme actually needs, and the maths follows: completion is measured against the scope you chose, not the method's full length.

  • Deferred phases read as "not part of this programme" — never as 0% or overdue
  • Phases open on the plan, all at once, or held back — the admin decides, and the decision is recorded
  • A phase closes when the work under it is finished — not when someone ticks a box

How it works

Five phases. Take what you need.

A structured path from "something is changing" to "it stuck" — with content, exercises and artifacts at every step, tailored to your industry.

PHASE 01

Diagnose

What is changing, who it lands on, and how ready they are today.

PHASE 02

Design

The change approach, the audiences, the impact ratings and the plan to close them.

PHASE 03

Engage

Communications, sponsors, champions — the work of bringing people with you.

PHASE 04

Embed

Training, go-live support and the readiness gates that decide if you go.

PHASE 05

Evaluate

Did it stick? Adoption, residual gaps and what the next programme should know.

Only need Diagnose, Design and Embed? Select those three. The other two are marked as out of scope everywhere they appear — and your completion percentage is calculated against what you actually signed up to do.

These five phases are structured around the process groups in the ACMP Standard for Change Management — assess impact and readiness, form the strategy, build the plan, execute it, close it out. They are not ADKAR and not Prosci's three phases, because ChangeFlow is a delivery platform, not a competing methodology. Run Prosci, Kotter or your own house model inside it — the phases are where your work lives, not a replacement for how you do it.
